The matching algorithm (not AI)

Matching is deterministic and rule-based: no machine learning, no model, no external service. Five weighted signals produce a score out of 100.

SignalWeightWhat it compares
Category{{ weights.category }}Identical category, or a related one (wallet ↔ bag)
Item name{{ weights.title }}Word overlap, plus a bonus when one name contains the other
Description{{ weights.description }}Shared identifying detail words
Location{{ weights.location }}Same place, or a place name contained in the other
Date{{ weights.date }}How many days apart the loss and the find happened
Total100

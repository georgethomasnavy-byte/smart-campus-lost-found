How it works

A simple process for everyone

Five weighted signals compare category, item name, description and identifying details, general area, and date. Exact find locations and claim answers stay private and are never used by the matcher. No AI or black box: each score is explainable.

  1. 1 ReportAdd item details with a photo.
  3. 2 MatchThe engine scores every opposite report.
  5. 3 Get notifiedYou’re alerted if a match is found.
  7. 4 ReuniteAdmin reviews ownership; finder confirms handover.
